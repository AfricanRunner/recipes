% Baked Salmon

# Ingredients

- 4 Salmon fillets (~6 oz each)
- 2 tbsp Olive oil
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1/4 tsp Cracked black pepper
- 2 tsp Minced garlic
- 1 tsp Italian herb seasoning
- 1 Lemon

# Method

1. Preheat oven to **400° F**. Arrange salmon fillets on a baking sheet and season generously with salt and pepper.

2. Stir together olive oil, garlic, herbs, and juice of 1/2 lemon. Spoon over the salmon, rubbing over the tops and sides so there are no dry spots. Thinly slice the remaining 1/2 lemon and top each fillet with a lemon slice.

3. Bake for **12–15 minutes** until the salmon is opaque and flakes apart with a fork. Broil the last **1–2 minutes** if desired.

4. Garnish with fresh thyme or parsley if desired and serve.
